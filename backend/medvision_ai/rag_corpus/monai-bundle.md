The MONAI brats_mri_segmentation bundle expects aligned T1 contrast-enhanced, T1, T2, and FLAIR channels, applies channel-wise nonzero intensity normalization, and produces tumor-region channels that are discretized into a labelmap. It is a research model bundle for four-channel glioma MRI segmentation. Bundle version, checkpoint checksum, inference configuration, and local runtime are recorded by this application in docs/BRAIN_TUMOR_AI_PLAN.md. The current application pins bundle version 0.5.4 and checkpoint SHA-256 860ccb3f1c21c99d0410ad8a1ac4ef6b8fab60cec0a503b0ba42675741a750ae.

The current implementation accepts co-registered BraTS-like, skull-stripped 1 mm isotropic volumes in RAS orientation. It requires all four modalities and rejects unsupported geometry rather than guessing registration. Predictions are research outputs; the bundle is not evidence of clinical validity for a new scan population. A single MSD Task01 evaluation case cannot establish generalization, calibration, or clinical accuracy.
