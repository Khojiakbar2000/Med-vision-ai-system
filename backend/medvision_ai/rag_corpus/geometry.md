NiBabel documents a NIfTI affine as the mapping from voxel array coordinates into millimeter world coordinates. The three spatial column vectors of its affine linear component describe voxel-axis directions and sizes. Physical voxel volume is the absolute determinant of the 3 by 3 spatial affine matrix in cubic millimeters; multiplying by a binary mask voxel count gives physical region volume, then divide by 1000 to report cubic centimeters. This calculation requires the mask and image to share the same spatial geometry. A voxel count alone is not a physical volume when voxel spacing differs.

For this application, predicted volume is computed from actual model-mask voxels and the source grid spacing. Ground-truth overlap scores are displayed only when an explicitly selected and geometry-matched reference mask is available. Missing ground truth means Dice and IoU are unavailable, not zero.
