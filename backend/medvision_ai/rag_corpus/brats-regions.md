BraTS label conventions and dataset protocols have changed across challenge years, so labels must be interpreted using the documentation for the specific release. For common BraTS conventions, whole tumor (WT) includes edema and all tumor core structures; tumor core (TC) includes non-enhancing and enhancing core but excludes edema; enhancing tumor (ET) is the enhancing core. The reviewed datasets use co-registered T1, contrast-enhanced T1, T2, and FLAIR MRI, while preprocessing and annotation details vary between releases. These region masks are research annotations, not a clinical diagnosis. Reported segmentation results depend on the release, preprocessing, annotations, and evaluation protocol.

The review discusses the evolution of BraTS datasets and annotation protocols; its CC BY 4.0 license permits reuse with attribution. Any benchmark score should be tied to its dataset version and evaluation protocol. A Dice score is not a probability of correctness and does not establish clinical accuracy; it is an overlap statistic for one specified prediction and reference.
